About Us

Two perspectives, one lens

Buma Venture sits at the intersection of two things that are rarely found together: a deep, top level understanding of how large organizations actually function, and a practical, current grasp of what AI can do inside them. Neither view is worth much on its own. The value is in the overlap.

Our management has hands on experience building large scale carve out companies, taking a function or business out of a parent organization and turning it into a standalone, fully operational company. That experience is directly relevant when an AI driven capability is ready to become a venture of its own.

Our engagements start as advisory work for top management and boards. But we don't stop at recommendations. When a project surfaces a process, tool, or capability that could stand on its own, we're positioned to help carve it out, build it into a SaaS product, and back it as an investor, turning a client's internal win into a new venture.
